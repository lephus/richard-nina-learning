**MỘT SỐ ĐỘNG TỪ KHÔNG CHIA TIẾP DIỄN (với cách dùng đang xảy ra ở thời điểm nói)**:\
• Verbs which express likes and dislikes:\
Example: like, love, hate, dislike, enjoy, want, wish, hope, prefer...\
•  Verbs of perception (động từ nhận thức)\
Example: believe = think (cho rằng -- bày tỏ quan điểm), know, notice, remember, forget, recognize (công nhận, thừa nhận, nhận ra), understand, realize (nhận ra),...\
•   Verbs of senses (động từ tri giác)\
Example: feel, hear, see, smell (có mùi), taste (có vị), sound (nghe có vẻ), look (trông có vẻ).\
•   Some other verbs: be, contain (chứa), fit (vừa), include (gồm), matter (có ý nghĩa -- it doesn't matter), need, belong (+to: thuộc về), cost, owe (nợ), appear (có vẻ ) = seem, have (nghĩa sở hữu, có -- I have a new laptop)

**CHÚ Ý CÁC VÍ DỤ SAU:\
1. I think** she's Italian. (=believe)\
**I'm thinking** about my holiday. ( = am considering)\
2. The soup **tastes** awful (= has an awful flavour)\
**She's tasting** the soup (= is testing the flavour of the soup)\
3. I can **see** an aeroplane in the sky (= perceive with my eyes)\
**I'm seeing** John tonight (= am meeting)\
4. Susan **looks** tired (= appears)\
**Susan is looking** at some photos.\
5. The room **smells** of perfume (= has the smell)\
The **cat is smelling** its food (= is sniffing)\
6. This towel **feels** soft (= has a soft texture)\
Jill **is feeling** her son's forehead (= touching)\
7. He **is** selfish (= character -- permanent state)\
He **is being** selfish (= behaviour -- temporary situation)

Yesterday, last night/ week/ month/..., two days/weeks/ months\... + ago, in + thời gian ở quá khứ (in 1990\...)

**PHÂN BIỆT "USED TO" -- "BE USED TO" -- "GET USED TO"**

- **Used to + Infinitive**: thói quen trong quá khứ, hiện tại không còn nữa.

- **Be used to/ Get used to + noun/ pronoun/ V-ing**: quen với ai/ điều gì

- They are used to hot weather.

- I'm used to getting up early.

- She wasn't used to working at night.

- Mary is getting used to life in the city.
